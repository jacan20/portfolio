James Cannon is a passionate Mechanical Engineering student at Auburn University, set to graduate in May 2025.

Beyond the classroom, James has served as Chief Engineer and Recovery Team Lead for the Auburn University Rocketry Association, where he’s played a key role in designing and integrating high power rockets for NASA Student Launch, Argonia Cup, and IREC competitions

His academic projects include the following:
-Senior Design: 12U SpaceTACSI Thermal Control System
-Applied Mechatronics
  -12v Brushed DC Motor
  -Analog Inverted Pendulum Controller
  -Digital Inverted Pendulum Controller (Planned)
  -Foosball Defender Arcade Game (Planned)
-Principles of Navigation and Guidence
  -Comparions of inertial navigation, GPS, sensor fusion, and vehicle dynamice simulations for a double lane change manuveur in MATLAB
-System Dynamics and Controls
  -Steering controller for an Autonomous Indie Car at 240 mph.
-Concepts of Mechanical Design
  -Autonomous golf ball launching and catching rover

His personal projects include the following:
-NAR L2 Certification Vehicle (in progress)
-NAR L1 Certification Vehicle
-N-body gravitataional simulation in p5.js
-Monte Carlo Dart-board Pi Approximator (Python)
-Various Basic Arduino and Raspberry Pi Projects


Skilled in Python, MATLAB, and LaTeX, and honored as an Eagle Scout as well as an active member of Pi Tau Sigma and Pi Gamma Tau, James brings a friendly, solutions-oriented mindset to every project. He’s eager to connect, collaborate, and create impactful engineering solutions that inspire progress and innovation.
