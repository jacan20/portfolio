James Cannon is a passionate Mechanical Engineering student at Auburn University, set to graduate in May 2025.

Beyond the classroom, James has served as Chief Engineer and Recovery Team Lead for the Auburn University Rocketry Association, where he has played a key role in designing and integrating high-power rockets for NASA Student Launch, Argonia Cup, and IREC competitions.

## Academic Projects

- **Senior Design:** 12U SpaceTACSI Thermal Control System
- **Applied Mechatronics:**
  - 12V Brushed DC Motor
  - Analog Inverted Pendulum Controller
  - Digital Inverted Pendulum Controller (Planned)
  - Foosball Defender Arcade Game (Planned)
- **Principles of Navigation and Guidance:**
  - Comparisons of inertial navigation, GPS, sensor fusion, and vehicle dynamics simulations for a double lane change maneuver in MATLAB
- **System Dynamics and Controls:**
  - Steering controller for an autonomous Indy Car at 240 mph
- **Concepts of Mechanical Design:**
  - Autonomous golf ball launching and catching rover

Skilled in Python, MATLAB, and LaTeX—and honored as an Eagle Scout, as well as an active member of Pi Tau Sigma and Pi Gamma Tau—James brings a friendly, solutions-oriented mindset to every project. He is eager to connect, collaborate, and create impactful engineering solutions that inspire progress and innovation.
