This is my high-power rocket for the Level 2 certification flight. It's built and waiting to fly.

## Vehicle Overview

- **Airframe:** 4-inch phenolic tube
- **Nosecone:** 3D printed
- **Fin can and motor mount:** a single 3D-printed polycarbonate part
- **Motor:** J270, for a planned apogee of about 2,500 ft
- **Recovery:** dual deploy, with two Altus Metrum EasyMini altimeters for redundancy

## 3D-Printed Fin Can

The fin can combines the fins and the motor mount in one polycarbonate part, printed on a Bambu Lab P1S. Polycarbonate is strong and heat resistant, but it shrinks as it cools, so large parts tend to warp off the build plate. Getting a clean print took a lot of trial and error. What finally worked was printing the fin can upside down.

## Recovery

The rocket uses dual deployment: a small drogue parachute deploys at apogee to bring the rocket down quickly, and the main parachute deploys closer to the ground for a soft landing. That keeps drift low while still landing gently. Two EasyMini altimeters provide redundancy, so a single altimeter failure won't prevent the parachutes from deploying.

## Status

The vehicle is complete and ready for its certification flight. I'll update this page with flight results.
